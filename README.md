# BILL-96-BUDDY

An interactive compliance guide for Quebec's Bill 96 (Law 14) — Quebec's major reform of its language laws that strengthens the status of French as the province's only official and "common" language and significantly expands French‑language requirements in government, business, education, and the workplace.

## What is Bill 96?

**Bill 96** is *"An Act respecting French, the official and common language of Québec,"* adopted by the National Assembly in May 2022 and brought into force in stages. Its formal name after passage is **Law 14**, and it amends the **Charter of the French Language (Bill 101)**.

Its stated purpose is to affirm that French is the **sole official language** of Quebec and the **common language** of the Quebec nation.

## Key Areas

### Government and Courts
- Stronger obligation for the civil administration to use French
- Tighter limits on using English or bilingual documents in dealings with the state
- Exceptions for: Indigenous peoples, services outside Quebec, tourism, and recent immigrants (short period)

### Language of Work
- Expands employees' right to work in French
- Requires employers to avoid unnecessary demands for knowledge of another language
- Extends application of the French Charter to smaller businesses (down from 50+ employees to **25+ employees**)

### Business and Commerce
- More stringent rules on French in commercial signage, advertising, contracts, and product labelling
- Clearer dominance of French on signs and marketing materials
- **New regulations effective June 1, 2025** for trademarks and product packaging
- Grace period until **June 1, 2027** for non-compliant products manufactured before that date
- **No grace period** for public signage

### Education
- Imposes caps/quotas on English-language CEGEP enrollment
- Adds French-language course requirements even for many students in English institutions

### Enforcement and Sanctions
- Strengthens powers of the **Office québécois de la langue française (OQLF)** to investigate, issue orders, and enforce compliance
- Increased fines substantially:
  - Companies: recurring non-compliance can reach up to **tens of thousands of dollars per day**
  - Individuals: fines scale up for repeat violations

## Using BILL-96-BUDDY

Open `index.html` in your browser to use the interactive guide. Select your role (employer, employee, business owner, student, or citizen) to get the specific Bill 96 obligations and risks that apply to you.
