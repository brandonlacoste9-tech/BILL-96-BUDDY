# Bill 96 Compliance Outreach Template (2026 Updated)

## Dual-Strike Email Strategy

For every HIGH RISK lead (score < 50), generate TWO email versions:

### Email 1: Personalized to Decision Maker
- Use owner/GM name in greeting
- Reference their specific business context (family business, multiple locations, etc.)
- Personal tone - peer to peer
- Emphasize reputation they've built
- File naming: `{business}-pitch-owner.txt`

### Email 2: General Management Team
- Professional but urgent tone
- Request forwarding to decision maker by name
- Include all contact info found
- More formal legal language
- File naming: `{business}-pitch-general.txt`

## Required Elements (Both Versions)

### Opening (Personalized vs General)
**Personalized:**
```
Bonjour [First Name],

En tant que [context about their business/role], vous comprenez...
```

**General:**
```
Bonjour à l'équipe de direction,

Veuillez transmettre ce message à [Owner Name] ou au directeur général...
```

### Body Structure

1. **Compliance Score** - State it clearly with risk level
2. **Specific Violations** - List 2-3 concrete technical issues found
3. **2026 Enforcement Context** - Mention "Inspecteur Incognito" initiative
4. **Financial Exposure** - State potential fines ($30,000 per violation)
5. **Location/Business Risk Factors** - Why they're a target

### The "Fix It" Hook (CRITICAL - Use This Exact Language)

```
Mon agence locale spécialisée en remédiation rapide de la Loi 14. Nous ne faisons pas que traduire - nous reconstruisons l'architecture pour que votre site serve automatiquement du contenu français conforme aux auditeurs de l'OQLF.

Nous pouvons corriger votre plateforme spécifique en moins de 48 heures pour un tarif forfaitaire, qui représente une fraction de la pénalité minimale de 3 000 $ de l'OQLF.

Voulez-vous que je vous envoie le rapport d'audit PDF gratuit montrant exactement où votre site échoue actuellement?
```

### Closing

**DO NOT include specific pricing in initial email**

Instead, offer the PDF report:
- Screenshots of code violations
- Line-by-line HTML analysis
- Detailed remediation plan
- Cost estimate vs potential fines (in the PDF, not the email)

### Call to Action

```
Disponible pour un appel rapide cette semaine ?
```

### P.S. (Urgency Reinforcement)

```
P.S. : L'OQLF utilise des outils automatisés pour scanner [their business type]. [Business Name] est probablement déjà dans leur base de données. Agir maintenant vous protège avant qu'une inspection formelle ne soit déclenchée.
```

## Pricing Strategy

### Email #1: NO PRICE
- Goal: Get them to reply "Yes, send the PDF"
- Micro-commitment before revealing cost

### PDF Report: INCLUDE PRICE
At bottom of PDF:
```
Plan de remédiation :
- Configuration multilingue complète : 1 500 $ - 2 500 $ (installation unique)
- Maintenance de conformité : 150 $/mois (optionnel)
- Comparez ceci à l'amende minimale de l'OQLF de 3 000 $

Délai : 48-72 heures
```

## Contact Information to Include

Always research and include:
- General email (info@, contact@, gm@)
- Phone number
- Owner/GM names (if found)
- Physical address (shows you're local)

## Tone Guidelines

- **Helpful, not threatening** - You're protecting them, not attacking
- **Technical but accessible** - Show expertise without jargon overload
- **Local expert positioning** - Emphasize Ouest-de-l'Île/Montreal knowledge
- **Urgency without panic** - Serious but solvable
- **Peer-level for owners** - Respectful for general inbox

## Language

- Primary: French (shows you practice what you preach)
- Include English version at bottom for internal reference
- Use proper Quebec French terminology (Loi 96, OQLF, prédominance marquée)

## Follow-Up Sequence (If No Response)

- Day 0: Send both emails
- Day 2: Phone call to general number
- Day 5: Follow-up email with subject "2ème avis - Lou's Pointe Claire"
- Day 10: Final email mentioning OQLF inspection timeline

## Success Metrics

- Open rate target: 60%+ (legal subject lines get opened)
- Reply rate target: 25%+ (fear + solution = action)
- Conversion target: 10%+ (1 in 10 HIGH RISK leads becomes client)
