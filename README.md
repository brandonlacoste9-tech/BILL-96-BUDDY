# BILL-96-BUDDY

Bill 96 is Quebec's major reform of its language laws that strengthens the status of French as the province's only official and "common" language and significantly expands French‑language requirements in government, business, education, and the workplace.

## About This App

**Bill 96 Buddy** is a plain-language interactive web guide to help you understand what Bill 96 (Law 14) means for you. Select your role — employer, employee, student, citizen, or software developer — to see the specific obligations and risks that apply to your situation.

### Key Features

- **Overview** of what Bill 96 is, its official name (Law 14), and what it amends (Charter of the French Language / Bill 101)
- **Expandable key areas**: Government & Courts, Language of Work, Business & Commerce, Education, and Enforcement & Sanctions
- **"What's Your Angle?" selector**: personalized obligations, risks of non-compliance, and key dates for employers, employees, students, citizens, and software developers / tech companies
